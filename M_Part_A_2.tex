\section{Overall Project Cash Flow Analysis}
    This part of the report focuses on a financial assessment of the business, guided by Engineering Decision-Making (EDM). It provides a forecast of the cash flow of our company for the first three years and evaluates potential risks by testing different scenarios. The marketing, production and logistics portfolios were used to give estimates on the operational budget.
