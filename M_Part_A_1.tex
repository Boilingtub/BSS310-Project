\section{Value chain design}
  \subsection{Primary Activities}
  \subsubsection{Inbound Logistics}
  This includes everything before production - receiving, storing, and distributing raw materials. Raw materials such as aluminium and nylon will be sourced from the most trustworthy and reputable local factories with materials that meet our lightweight, durability and affordability requirements. A good transport company with enclosed trucks for aluminium, and flatbed trucks for nylon rolls will be used to ensure on time delivery and no damage occurred during transit. Indirectly, we will use a just-in-time (JIT) inventory system. This system consists of very little to no excess stock and produces as materials are received to avoid wasting money on inventory costs. There is indeed a very small strategic safety stock for possible trends, supporting affordable manufacturing. A manufacturing facility will be almost evenly placed between both the nylon and aluminium suppliers to ensure that both items are available at the same time as they come together in 1 package. This will ultimately ensure good lead times. Any excess raw materials will be stored in a small storage area right next to the manufacturing facility. There is no need for a separate big warehouse as we use a JIT system, and our product does not take up a lot of space.
  \subsubsection{Operations}
  The direct operations will prioritize cost-effective and high-quality production while also focusing on the professional and discreet design. This will be essential throughout production to achieve a competitive advantage. The manufacturing will be done in the manufacturing facility (as stated in 4.1.1).Production will rely on industrial machines such as milling and drilling machines, fabric cutters and sewing machines to optimise product fabrication.An automated TIG welder will be used to join aluminium parts ,producing durable joints .Focus will be given to eliminate any parts of the product that might cause noise, ensuring a noise free product across the whole manufacturing process. Indirectly quality assurance will be of a high standard by using autonomation(human checking of machined products).The factory will be set up using the 5S methodology of lean manufacturing, the main focus being standardization .A Standardized factory leads to better lead times as well as consistent production .Finished goods will only be stored until order quantity is achieved(very short time) .It will be stored in its own designated space within the storage area together with the excess raw materials
  \subsubsection{Outbound Logistics}
  Outbound logistics focus on distributing the product through trusted retail and online channels such as Clicks or Takealot in small nylon bags. The nylon bag’s compact design meets the easily transportable objective, reinforcing the portability value proposition. Direct activities involve good relationships with distribution partners to ensure wide availability and on-time delivery. The logistics of our whole supply chain is the most important at this stage. Online orders will be mostly via the retail stores individual websites and thus it will be part of the same delivery as the in-store stock we deliver. Indirectly, smart labelling and setup guides (e.g. - QR codes) enhance the user experience, making the product easy to assemble. To ensure that there is never a miscommunication between us and the retail stores we will do demand forecasting. We’ll use exponential smoothing forecasts that prioritize recent sales data to anticipate ADHD market growth that might occur as our product gets recognised, while respecting JIT limits. This balances the possible usage growth with the lesser stock a JIT system has, protecting our affordability goals. These forecasts will help us ensure that there is no stock-outs but rather ensure a agile supply chain.
  \subsubsection{Marketing and sales}
  Marketing efforts directly target professionals through appropriate digital platforms such as Takealot or Temu, and in-store displays, highlighting the discreet and portable design aspects of the product. The ADHD solution will be the main focus when advertising. Focus will be applied to schools as well as universities as this is where young professionals first started struggling with ADHD. Indirectly, we will monitor buyer behaviour and support long-term sales strategies, including bulk orders for corporate wellness programs. These combined efforts reinforce the professional-market positioning of the product.
  \subsubsection{Service}
  Our service strategy’s main focus is around ensuring a professional, seamless, and supportive customer experience. Direct services include detailed setup guides accessible through QR codes on the packaging, along with a responsive customer support line to ensure customer satisfaction. This is very important as ADHD individuals will get bored very easily if the instructions aren’t clear. Indirectly, our after-sales service includes regular check-ins via chat-bots in WhatsApp or email with set-up tips, usage suggestions, and optional feedback forms to improve future products. Additionally, tutorial videos will be available on our website and social media, allowing users to engage with the product in their own time and at their own pace. This reinforces the value we place in easy set-up as well as customer satisfaction.
  \subsection{Support Activities}
  \subsubsection{Procurement}
  Procurement focuses on good supplier relationships to ensure affordable and high-quality materials. Directly, suppliers for aluminium and nylon are chosen based on long-term cost efficiency, reliability, and their ability to meet our quality standards for lightweight and durable materials. We prioritize local suppliers (within 50km) capable of daily deliveries, with performance metrics tracking above 95% on-time fulfilment. Indirectly, procurement activities involve contract negotiation. This is where the best possible prices will be negotiated. Our approved supplier list maintains 3 vetted options per material. These activities support the company’s affordability and reliable supply objectives. This reduces the impact of potential supplier price hikes or disruptions. The amount of raw material needed for a specific amount of finished product must be calculated to avoid ordering more than the actual request from the retailers.
  \subsubsection{Technological development}
  Our technology strategy aims to improve the product and production process while supporting the goals of easy setup and professional design. Directly, we will optimize our production system and use software, such as Miller Welds, for automated TIG welding to ensure durable and consistent products. As part of the autonomation, inspectors will be placed to over watch machines and spot possible defects. Indirectly, we will stay up to date on the latest lean manufacturing technology. This ensures we don’t mis any improvement opportunities. In this way we gain a competitive advantage over other companies.
  \subsubsection{Human resources management}
   Directly, we will hire workers with skills in lean manufacturing and suitable experience. “Workers should not work-they should think” (Taiichi Ohno). Machines such as fabric cutters, sewing machines, and welding equipment must be observed from time to time. Thus, we apply autonomation where machines and people are utilized together. Indirectly, we will partner with local technical colleges to create a pipeline of trained interns and future employees. We will then require a potential employee to complete a 1-month boot-camp. It will be a probationary period where all the skills they may need will be taught or strengthened. This not only ensures skilled labourers, but also supports our goals of affordable manufacturing and long-term success through workforce development.
  \subsubsection{Infrastructure}
   The facility will contain industrial machinery, such as milling and drilling machines, fabric cutters, sewing stations, and automated TIG welders. As stated earlier, the main building will be the manufacturing facility with a small storage room next to it.The physical infrastructure includes facilities planning that ensures efficient, standardized production, and fast order fulfilment.We will do facilities planning that ensures efficient, standardized production, and fast order fulfilment.
